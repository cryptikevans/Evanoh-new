One selector, two sides

Every booking is built to the room — from a wedding first dance to a warehouse until sunrise. Here's the full rundown, split like a record.

Side A — On the night

A1

Weddings & private parties

Ceremony to send-off, one sound and one selector who reads the guest list, not just the genre.

A2

Club nights & residencies

Weekly or one-off sets built for the floor, with a crate deep enough to hold a whole season.

A3

Corporate & brand events

Launches, after-parties and activations — sound that matches the brief without losing the groove.

A4

Festivals & outdoor stages

Big-rig sets built for open air, long runtimes and a crowd that's come from all over.

B1

Sound system hire

Rig, decks and lighting sized to the venue, delivered and run by the same person who books it.

B2

MC & hosting

A voice on the mic that keeps the night moving between sets, announcements and shout-outs.

B3

Mix & radio sessions

Recorded sets for radio slots, podcasts or your own channel — mixed live, delivered mastered.

B4

Music consultancy

Curating a playlist, a launch soundtrack or an in-store rotation — the ear, without the deck.
